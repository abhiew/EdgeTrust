# User Stories & Operational Workflows

## Story 1: Reviewing High-Risk AI Collections Recommendations
**As an** Operations Manager,  
**I want to** review high-risk loan repayment accounts in a 3-column workbench with verifiable evidence and policy check flags,  
**So that** I can approve or edit drafted customer communications before any outreach is dispatched.

## Story 2: Enforcing Zero-Tolerance Safety Policies
**As a** Compliance Manager,  
**I want to** configure automated policy rules (e.g. blocking threatening language or contacting opted-out customers),  
**So that** any non-compliant draft is instantly intercepted and flagged for human review.

## Story 3: Evaluating Agent Version Release Candidates
**As an** AI Product Manager,  
**I want to** test Collections Conversation Agent v2.4.1 against 100 benchmark scenarios and compare metrics side-by-side with v2.3.0,  
**So that** I can verify higher safety and grounding scores before promoting to staging.

## Story 4: Auditing Operational Actions for Regulatory Readiness
**As a** Governance Auditor,  
**I want an** append-only audit trail logging every user decision, timestamp, actor ID, and correlation ID,  
**So that** I can export verifiable CSV reports for regulatory compliance reviews.

## Story 5: Emergency Containment during Model Drift
**As a** COO / AI Product Manager,  
**I want to** pause an agent or force 100% human review clearance during an incident,  
**So that** operational risks are contained instantly without interrupting manual collections.
