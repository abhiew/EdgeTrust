# EdgeTrust Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: Core SaaS Control Tower (Current Release)
- Synthetic demo workspace with deterministic AI simulator.
- Agent Registry & version health monitoring.
- Operations 3-column Workbench & Approval Queue.
- Compliance Policy Engine & Immutable Audit Log.
- Evaluation Studio & side-by-side version comparison.
- Incident Management & Executive Reports generator.

## Phase 2: Enterprise Event Ingestion & Adapters
- Real-time event stream ingestion via Kafka / Webhooks.
- Multi-provider adapters (Azure OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Bedrock).
- Configurable visual policy rule builder.
- SSO (SAML 2.0 / OAuth2) & enterprise RBAC integration.

## Phase 3: Advanced Drift & Human Feedback Loop
- Automated model drift detection & bias monitoring.
- Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) preference learning.
- Automated cost & token usage optimizer.
- Multi-agent simulation sandbox environment.

## Phase 4: Enterprise Ecosystem Integrations
- Connectors for Salesforce, Zendesk, Finacle, Tally, and WhatsApp Business API.
- Multi-agent orchestration engine.
- Board-level governance compliance reports.
